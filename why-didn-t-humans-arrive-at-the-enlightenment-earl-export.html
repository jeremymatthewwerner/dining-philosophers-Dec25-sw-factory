Abd al-Rahman Ibn Khaldun (1332-1406) was a Tunisian historiographer and social scientist who lived through the political upheavals of medieval North Africa and Andalusia. He served various rulers as a diplomat and judge while developing his groundbreaking theories about historical patterns and social organization. His masterwork, the Muqaddimah, established him as a founder of sociology and historiography.

William Blake

William Blake (1757-1827) was an English poet, painter, and printmaker who created some of the most visionary works in Western art and literature. He lived through the height of the Enlightenment and Industrial Revolution while remaining deeply critical of both movements. Blake developed his own complex mythology and philosophy that synthesized Christianity, mysticism, and radical politics.
